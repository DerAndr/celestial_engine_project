Deterministic Binary UDP v2 Framing & Network Topology

The smartphone (Google Pixel 9 Pro XL running Android 15) functions as an autonomous Wi-Fi Hotspot (SoftAP) at 10.169.248.1. The Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W connects upon boot via its onboard Infineon CYW43439 802.11n radio. Both devices communicate over bidirectional UDP sockets on port 5005 with custom zero-copy framing, achieving round-trip latency under 18 milliseconds.

Dual Programmable I/O (PIO) I2S Audio Pipelines on RP2350

Celestial Engine Mk.V implements full-duplex digital audio without an external codec. The RP2350 ARM Cortex-M33 utilizes two independent PIO state machines and four DMA channels to stream microphone input and speaker output simultaneously at 16,000 Hz sample rate.

1. Microphone Input (INMP441) PIO0 SM0
  • SCK (Clock): GPIO 8 (Pico Pin 11)
  • WS (Word Select): GPIO 9 (Pico Pin 12)
  • SD (Serial Data): GPIO 10 (Pico Pin 14)
  • Format: 16 kHz, 16-bit Mono PCM (Left Channel)
  • DMA Double-Buffering: 2x 256-sample ping-pong buffers with zero CPU overhead.
2. Speaker Output (MAX98357A) PIO0 SM1
  • DIN (Serial Data): GPIO 0 (Pico Pin 1)
  • BCLK (Bit Clock): GPIO 1 (Pico Pin 2)
  • LRC / WS (Word Select): GPIO 2 (Pico Pin 4)
  • Power: 3.2W Class-D into 4Ω neodymium speaker (5V VBUS rail)
  • DMA Streamer: 16-bit signed PCM fed directly into 32-bit I2S serializer.
3. Onboard Hardware Chiptune Synthesizer (Zero-Network Fallback) Core 1 Dedicated

When network connectivity is unavailable, RP2350 Core 1 synthesizes authentic retro arcade square-wave chiptunes directly in C. The synthesizer computes phase accumulators at 16,000 samples/sec, mixing note frequencies, ADSR envelopes, and octave harmonics into the DMA ring buffer. Includes 24 iconic tracks (DOOM E1M1, Tetris, Star Wars Imperial March, Zelda, Daft Punk, etc.) with instant early stop and track skipping.

Dual-Brain Architecture & Autonomous Fault-Tolerant Redundancy

Celestial Engine Mk.V solves the classic wearable robotics problem: high-end neural networks consume too much power to reside in the handheld staff, but cloud-only reliance introduces lag and connectivity failure in the field. Mk.V uses a tiered Dual-Brain Architecture.

Brain 1: Google Pixel 9 Pro XL

Android 15 Compose

Primary interactive companion. Features the Eye of the Oracle camera for real-time computer vision, Gemma 4 multimodal AI reasoning, Arcane Grimoire spellbook, and Google Cloud neural TTS playback.

  • Operates as local Wi-Fi Hotspot router (zero external router required).
  • Instant touch screen UI for selecting spells, visual tuning, and live telemetry.
  • Dispatches commands to Pico 2 W in < 18ms.

Brain 2: Wearable Raspberry Pi 5 16GB

100% Offline Edge AI

Backpack-mounted autonomous edge compute node with a 1TB Lexar NM790 PCIe Gen 3 NVMe SSD. Runs completely offline without internet or cellular connectivity.

  • Whisper.cpp: Local speech-to-text inference (< 200ms).
  • Ollama LLM: Local Llama 3.2 1B & Gemma 2B quantization.
  • Piper Neural TTS: Fast C++ voice generation with arcane pitch models.
⚡ Autonomous Fail-Safe Core (Pico 2 W Firmware)

If both the Android phone and the Pi 5 server lose Wi-Fi signal, the staff's RP2350 microcontroller immediately detects packet timeout via its 1000ms watchdog. It smoothly transitions the HUD to AUTONOMOUS STANDALONE MODE: the physical rotary knob, OLED menu, 21 LED animations, and 24 retro chiptune synthesizers remain 100% operational with zero freeze or reboot.
