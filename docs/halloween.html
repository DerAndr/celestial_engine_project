Halloween DIY Prop // Not a Serious Production Device!

Please Note: This is a fun, lighthearted DIY prop crafted especially for Halloween! A cyber-arcane artifact housed inside a transparent plastic bell jar packed with colorful glowing styrofoam diffuser beads. Inside, you can openly see the solderless breadboard with the Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W, a vibrant rainbow bundle of DuPont jumper wires, a cybernetic OLED screen, and a large tactile yellow rotary knob at the base.

Please do not judge or evaluate this project as an industrial enterprise device or commercial hardware! It is an eclectic mix of arcane fantasy, 8-bit DOOM E1M1 chiptune synthesis, foam beads, exposed wires, wearable Raspberry Pi 5 in a backpack, and on-device Gemma 4 E2B / Gemini intelligence — built purely for maker curiosity, Halloween retro-futuristic vibes, and pure geeky fun! 👻

📷 Real Physical Artifact

Physical Build
Celestial Flask Build: Transparent bell dome with styrofoam bead light diffuser, 43 concentric LEDs (cyan/purple cosmic vortex), Pico 2 W on breadboard, 1.3" OLED display, and bottom yellow rotary knob.
Top Bell Dome: 43 WS2812B LEDs packed in pastel styrofoam beads (ethereal light diffuser)
Center Compartment: Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W on solderless breadboard with DuPont harness
Cybernetic OLED: SH1106 1.3" display in lower-right displaying "TURN: TUNE | OK"
Tactile Yellow Knob: Bourns PEC11R rotary encoder mounted through the base
